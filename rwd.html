About Me

My name is Ethan Autrey, and I am currently a student at Sam Houston State University pursuing a Bachelor of Science degree in Geospatial Science with a minor in Management Information Systems. I enjoy learning about geographic information systems, mapping, and technology. During my education, I have gained experience working with GIS software, spatial data, and different mapping projects. In my free time, I enjoy spending time with friends and family, playing video games, and learning new things. After graduation, I hope to begin a career where I can use my GIS skills and continue developing my knowledge in the field.
